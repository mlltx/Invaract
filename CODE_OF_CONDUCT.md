# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the Invaract community, in any capacity, will be treated with respect and dignity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- **Using welcoming and inclusive language** that acknowledges and celebrates diversity
- **Being direct and constructive** in technical discussions and code review
- **Focusing on what is best** for the community and the project
- **Showing empathy** towards other community members
- **Accepting constructive criticism** gracefully and offering it thoughtfully
- **Acknowledging mistakes** and learning from feedback

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment** in any form: sexual, discriminatory, or otherwise
- **Abuse, threats,** or inflammatory language
- **Personal attacks** or ad hominem arguments
- **Unwanted sexual attention** or physical contact
- **Deliberate intimidation** or stalking (online or offline)
- **Sustained disruption** of meetings, discussions, or events
- **Publishing private information** without consent
- **Discrimination** based on protected characteristics (race, gender, age, disability, etc.)
- **Bad-faith participation** intended solely to disrupt the community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Project communication channels (if established: Slack, Discord, mailing lists)
- Project events (meetups, conferences, virtual sessions)
- Social media when representing the project
- Private communications that relate to project participation

This Code of Conduct applies to project maintainers and contributors alike, both inside and outside project spaces.

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers:

**Primary contact:** Contact the project via GitHub issues or discussions with subject line: "CODE OF CONDUCT VIOLATION"

**Maintainers** will review reports privately and confidentially.

### What We Will Not Tolerate

- Retaliation against reporters
- Ignoring or downplaying reports
- Lack of transparency in investigations

### Possible Outcomes

Enforcement actions may include:

1. **Conversation**: Private discussion to clarify expectations
2. **Warning**: Written notice that behavior violates Code of Conduct
3. **Temporary suspension**: Temporary removal from project spaces (hours to days)
4. **Permanent ban**: Removal from project community

Decisions will be made by project maintainers, focusing on:
- Severity of the incident
- Pattern of behavior (first-time vs. repeated violations)
- Intent vs. impact
- Willingness to make amends
